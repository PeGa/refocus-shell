focus nudge — check the reminder system

  focus nudge status    is it on, and what's scheduled
  focus nudge test      fire a test notification, confirm it lands

Nudging isn't toggled here — it's armed by 'focus enable' and silenced by
'focus disable'. These two are just diagnostics.

The reminder fires every NUDGE_INTERVAL minutes, in every state:
  focusing → "Focusing on: X (Nm)"
  paused   → "Paused: X"
  idle     → "Not focusing on anything."

test is where you debug a notification that isn't showing (DISPLAY, Wayland, DBus).
